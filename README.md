## The entry (94 characters, one line)

> I finally met my soulmate — turns out she's my reflection, and even she's seeing someone else.

## Why it works

The setup promises a love story ("finally met my soulmate"), then the turn flips it: the soulmate is my own reflection — and the punchline twists once more, because even she is seeing someone else. Self-deprecation, surprise, and every word earning its place in 94 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no clock) gate-checks the line (1–200 chars, single line, no leading/trailing whitespace) and writes it — with no trailing newline — to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`). Two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
